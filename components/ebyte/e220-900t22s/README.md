fitted on the [M5Stack LoRa Unit U170](../../../devices/m5stack/unit-lora-e220/README.md),
is documented together with its 433 MHz sibling in:

## → [`components/ebyte/e220-400t22s/README.md`](../e220-400t22s/README.md)

a future pass may need somewhere to put a `E220-900T22S`-specific datasheet. The shared
record covers architecture, the E220-vs-E22 distinction, and why neither is a Meshtastic
path.
