## Evidence notes

- **The v1.1 delta rests almost entirely on V-08 vs V-09** — two schematics, parsed and diffed.
  The vendor's own prose (V-01, V-07) agrees with the schematic reading on the RGB-LED change and
  the antenna change, and **contradicts** it on the DC-DC part.
- **V-03, V-04 and V-12 are negative findings established by hashing**, not by reading. They are
  the reason this record is scoped as a revision document rather than a second full record.
- **V-15 is a negative finding established by grep.** It is the load-bearing evidence for the
  predicted RGB-LED failure, and it is a source read — not a test.
- **Nothing in this record was verified on v1.1 hardware.**
