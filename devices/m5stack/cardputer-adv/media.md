# Cardputer ADV — images and provenance

> Snapshot **2026-09-04**. Machine-readable form: [`media/manifest.json`](media/manifest.json).

## Rights position

**No M5Stack product photograph or render is retained in this repository.**
[`vendors/m5stack`](../../../vendors/m5stack/README.md) §8 records that M5Stack product imagery
carries **no redistribution licence**; the PaperMono pass reached the same conclusion and
retained none either. Redistribution status for every vendor image below is therefore
**`unknown`** and disposition **`reference-only`** — URL, description and provenance recorded,
bytes not committed.

**Schematic sheet renders** are a different case only in degree: they are also unlicensed, but
they are *technical* documents rather than marketing assets, and reading them was necessary to
produce this record. They were fetched into **scratch** (which lives in the separate archive
repository), used to make the crops that every transcribed value came from, and **not** committed
here. The schematic **PDF** itself *is* retained under `artifacts/schematic/`, matching how this
repository already treats M5Stack schematics elsewhere.

## Images that would improve this record

| Wanted | Why |
|---|---|
| Underside photo showing the **CAP header** with a scale reference | the only way to confirm the physical pin-1 marking against the three numbering schemes |
| **PCB photograph, board removed** | would let a reader confirm the designator census independently and settle U1–U4 |
| **Silkscreen revision marking**, v1.0 vs v1.1 | would settle conflict C3 |
| A 1:1 crop of ADV sheet 3 around **U7 pins 4 and 9** | would settle whether the BMI270 interrupts are routed |
| A 1:1 crop of ADV sheet 1 around **P1** | would settle the Grove SCL/SDA conflict |

The last two need no new hardware or rights — only another pass over PNGs already identified.
