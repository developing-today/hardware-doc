# Xteink X4 Light — market and pricing

*Created 2026-09-11 by the round-2 index merge.*

## There is no pricing, and there is no product page. That is the finding.

The X4 Light is **certified but not sold**. It was found by enumerating Xteink's FCC grantee
code `2BTR9`, not by any commercial signal, and as of **2026-09-11** it has no storefront
presence of any kind.

| Channel | Result, 2026-09-11 |
|---|---|
| `xteink.com` `/products.json?limit=250&page=1` | **25 products enumerated, complete.** No X4 Light, under any spelling |
| `xteink.com` `/collections.json` | **No `x4-light` collection** — unlike the X4 Classic, which had a provisioned empty collection weeks before launch |
| X4 Light–specific accessories | **none** |
| AliExpress / Amazon / eBay / Taobao / JD | **not obtained** — all bot-walled or JS-shelled from the research host on 2026-09-11. Not an absence finding |
| Community (Reddit, GitHub) | no mention located |

**Compare the X4 Classic's pre-launch signature.** On 2026-09-04 the X4 Classic already had a
provisioned `x4-classic` collection containing accessories, five days before it went on sale. The
X4 Light has **nothing** — not even an empty collection. On that evidence a launch is **not
imminent**, though the FCC grant says one is intended.

## The only dated commercial fact that exists

**None.** Every row above is an absence.

The one dated *regulatory* fact is in
[`certification-and-compliance.md`](certification-and-compliance.md): FCC `2BTR9-X4L`, granted
**2026-08-13**, applicant Shenzhen Xiaohu Xingtong Technology Co., Ltd., test firm Shenzhen BKC
Testing Co., Ltd.

## Caveats

- **No marketplace channel was sampled.** The absences above are vendor-store absences only.
- A granted FCC authorisation is a **strong signal of commercial intent** — it costs real money
  — but it is **not** a commitment to ship. Products are certified and cancelled.
