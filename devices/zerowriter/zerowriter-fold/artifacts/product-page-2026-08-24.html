    The next Zerowriter

    A writing tool that keeps work in focus.

    Fold open and get going. Zerowriter Fold is a purpose-built e-paper word processor in a slim, laptop-like enclosure. Featuring a frontlit 6" e-ink display, a hot-swappable mechanical keyboard, SD-card storage, and weeks of battery life.

    A simple modern day word processor. Zerowriter Fold makes plain text files. So that means it can hold millions of your words with no trouble.

    Other options are out there. Don’t pay for bloat. This was built for writers looking for simplicity and security.

    Everything you need. Distraction-free.

    Built for your writing life.

    Fast 6" e-ink display with frontlight

    Clarity without compromise. No perceptible typing delay. Exceptional contrast in any lighting, and a built-in screen light keeps you going after dark. Gentle on the eyes, hard to put down.

    Low-profile mechanical keyboard

    A writing tool should feel good. Custom keycaps, hot-swappable Choc switches, and a standard 60% layout. Choose your switches: quiet, tactile, or whatever suits your style.

    Weeks of battery life. Instant on.

    Open it and write. Your Zerowriter picks up exactly where you left off, no boot, no loading. E-ink power consumption means weeks of writing between charges, wherever you take it.

    Goes everywhere you do

    A folding enclosure that protects the screen and keyboard when closed, and adjusts to any angle when open. It slips into your bag and comes out ready to write.

    Built on trust

    Software that respects you.

    Zerowriter Fold is a high-trust tool. Here’s what that means in practice.

    • No subscriptions. Venture capitalists hate us.

      Buy it once, own it forever. Zerowriter Fold works with no ongoing fees, no hooks, no gotchas.

    • Open source at the core

      Built on Zerowriter Core, open source software the world can read and verify. No funny business. Community supported, no matter what happens to us.

    • Your files, your way

      Saves to micro-SD, like a camera. Plug in via USB-C to manage files directly. Simple, transparent, and entirely yours.

    • Two modes. One tool.

      Choose Drafting Mode for an always-forward, distraction-free experience. Switch to Word Processing Mode for full editing, bookmarks, and document management. Your call.

    • In development: Cloud Push

      Optionally send files to your personal Google Drive over wifi. Coming soon.

    A small team that builds great tools.

    We’ve done this before.

    We started in 2024 with a viral DIY project and a Raspberry Pi Zero (hence, the name). In ’25 and ’26, we’ve shipped Zerowriter Ink to writers around the world — and we still are! We learned everything about building consumer electronics the hard way and developed our own underlying display technologies, boards, and processes.

    Now our team in Ottawa, Ontario is ready with the first fully-realized Zerowriter. Each device assembled by hand and personally tested in-house. It’s been a difficult year for Canadian businesses. We’re proud to build as much as we can at home and build real jobs in a traditional tech-forward city.

    Production capacity exceeds 300 units per month and assembly supports hard-working Canadian workers. Our first units will be ready to roll forward in late 2026. We’ll be fulfilling this campaign first-in, first-out.

    Zerowriter Fold is a finished production prototype. The next steps are finalizing manufacturing, parts procurement, and assembly. First orders are expected to ship in late 2026. First Wave backers ship first. First in, first out.
    We’re fulfilling on a first-come, first-serve basis. Those who order earliest get their Zerowriter Fold earliest. We’re splitting fulfillment into waves to keep things moving, so backers can expect an ongoing process rather than one big ship date. Signing up now holds your spot and your First Wave price. There’s no payment required until the campaign goes live.
    Yes. Zerowriter Fold creates plain text .txt and .md files, which are compatible with any modern operating system. Write on the Fold, then transfer your files to any computer for further editing if you like. No drivers, no proprietary software required.
    The keyboard uses Choc v1 switches. They are user-replaceable and hot-swappable. No soldering required. There are dozens of Choc-compatible switches available with a range of typing profiles and feels. We’ll be featuring some enthusiast switch options when we launch.
    Zerowriter Fold runs on Zerowriter Core, written in Arduino and releasing as open source in the coming months. Open source means that even if the company isn’t around someday, people can still build, modify, and develop for the Fold. Your work and writing is secure and stable, and our software can be kept in-check and improved by hundreds or thousands of developers around the world.

All of it affects battery life, and it varies significantly person to person. A slow, thoughtful writer with their screen light off will see very different numbers than someone working away with the frontlight on full.
